Why Collars Push and Pipe Pulls

The Neutral Point — where tension becomes compression
Max Weight on Bit
WOB (lb) = Collar wt (lb/ft) × Length (ft) × BF
BF = 1 − (Mud Weight (ppg) ÷ 65.5)  —  keeps the neutral point in the collars, not the pipe.
Example — 900 ft of collars, 12 ppg mud
Buoyancy Factor1 − (12 ÷ 65.5) = 0.817
Collar weight147 lb/ft × 900 ft
Max WOB before pipe goes into compression≈ 108,100 lb
